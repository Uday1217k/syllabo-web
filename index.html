Privacy Policy (India DPDP Act 2023 Compliant)

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 | Platform: Syllabo by Saathi

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data required to provide your study account: your mobile number or email address for OTP verification, full name, date of birth (to verify age band), educational stream, and your study syllabi/notes.

2. Under-18 Verifiable Guardian Consent

In strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), students under 18 years of age are required to provide Parent/Guardian Name, Guardian Email, and explicit Guardian Consent during profile onboarding before accessing cloud features.

3. Private Grounded Chapter Mode & Community Vault

Documents uploaded in Private Grounded Mode are scoped strictly to your account. If you opt in to contribute notes to the Community Knowledge Vault, all personal identifiers (emails, phone numbers, roll numbers) are automatically stripped prior to distillation.

4. Data Portability & Right to Erasure

You can export 100% of your data as JSON (GET /api/export) or permanently purge your account and all associated cloud records (DELETE /api/account) at any time from the Settings & Privacy tab inside the app.
